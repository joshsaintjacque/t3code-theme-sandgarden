Sandgarden

A dry rock garden for your agent chat. Raked sand, quiet stones and moss ink. While the agent works, a rake draws furrows down the margins. When it stops, they settle into ripples and the sand goes still.

InstallWatch the demo

What moves, and why

Nothing animates for its own sake. Every movement is a rake doing a job, and each one keys off something the app already knows.

  • At restThe furrows are set and the ripples sit still around each stone. Nothing moves.
  • A turn is runningMoss furrows are raked into each margin over about twenty seconds (about ten in the narrower 72px strips), bending round the stones and replacing the sand lines. Then the garden holds with a slow ring breath of 7%, so a long turn stays calm and nothing resets.
  • Working rowThree wavy furrows flow under the live Working line.
  • A background thread is busyThe same wavy furrows run, slower, under that thread in the sidebar.
  • Composer focusTwo soft ripples spread out from the composer's edge.
  • Hover a threadThree tines are drawn under the row, each stopping a little short of the last.
  • Turn completeThe moss fades into the sand over about a second and a half while each set of ripples spreads from its own stone. A turn that ends early fades only what was drawn. Then the sand goes still.
  • Narrow windowWhen the margins are under about 220px (a small window, or the details panel open) a slim strip with one stone takes over, and turns moss while a turn runs. The header garden and the composer's furrows do the same.
  • Reduced motionEvery animation stops. A running turn shows the finished moss garden as a still, and busy rows keep their wavy furrows so state is still visible.

One turn, start to finish

Idle, compose, a turn starts, a background thread goes busy, the answer lands, hover, and the narrow-window fallback.

Install

One JSON file. No fonts, images or extra files to download.

In the app

Copy the theme, then open Settings › Appearance › Add theme and paste.

Download

From the terminal

Fetch the file and set it with the T3 CLI.

curl -fsSL -o sandgarden.json https://joshsaintjacque.github.io/t3code-theme-sandgarden/sandgarden.json
~/.t3/bin/t3 theme set sandgarden.json

Light theme with margin art on windows 1460px wide and up; narrower layouts keep a slim one-stone strip in each margin. Source: GitHub · MIT.
